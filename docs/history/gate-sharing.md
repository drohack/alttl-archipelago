# Ability gates and what bypasses them

Generated by `tools/probe-object-sharing.py` from DevTools'
`sharing:` dump of which controllers hold which objects. The dimmer
merges per object and lets UNLOCKED WIN among the groups that act on it,
so a gated group is bypassed whenever every object it holds is also held
by a group the player can already touch. A drawer or cupboard does not
count: it holds what sits inside it and frees nothing another group holds
(`ObjectLock`).

**Since 2026-09-25 the 28 rows of "Bypassed once the player holds something
else" whose Bypassed by column reads Drawer no longer apply.** All 18 levels
with a drawer or cupboard were re-swept that day: those rows are exactly the
groups a drawer was freeing, and the dimmer now holds them.
The rest of this report predates that rule; `tools/probe-object-sharing.py`
now models it.

Swept 82 multi-controller level(s).

## CONFIRMED BY HAND, AND APPLIED, 2026-09-20

Eight of the nine below were played by droha and are corrected in
levels.json via `bypassedAbilities`. That field feeds the REQUIREMENT view
only - `enforced_abilities` in data.py - and never the draw, because
test_regression freezes the 0.3.4 random stream and positional ids make a
seed in flight a contract with a mod that is no longer running.

NOT APPLIED, and why - one level, and it is not testable this way:

- **DLC2 Math Set** (Ordering) - cannot be hand-tested this way. Its
  Indexables group reports solved=True the moment the level loads, so there
  is no arrangement left to perform and nothing to observe.

WHAT THE TESTING SETTLED ABOUT THE CLASSES. The first guess was that
HangingToolsController and Indexables should not map to an ability at all.
That is wrong and would have done real damage: HangingToolsController
appears in FIVE levels and only three are toothless - DLC1 Bathroom
Cupboard and DLC2 Whistles gate properly. Indexables appears in ELEVEN and
only four are toothless. The mapping is right; it fails only where a
baseline Draggables group happens to share every object, which is a
per-level fact and needs a per-level override.

the listed ability arrives - which is why a zero-ability sweep cannot
see them. Spoons is the known example: its Ordering group shares all
seven spoons with a Stacking group, and droha completed it, earning
the Ordering-gated location, while holding only Stacking.
